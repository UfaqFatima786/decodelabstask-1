A Letter to My Future Self

Dear Future Me,

I wonder where life has taken you by the time you're reading this.
Right now, I'm still learning, growing, and figuring things out one step at a time. I'm balancing my studies, learning web development, building projects, and chasing dreams that sometimes feel far away but never impossible.
I hope you've become the doctor you've always wanted to be. I hope you've continued exploring psychology and understanding people more deeply. I hope you've kept your curiosity alive and never stopped learning, no matter how busy life became.
I hope you've built strong skills, created meaningful projects, and found ways to earn through your knowledge and hard work. More than success, I hope you've found freedom—the freedom to make your own choices, follow your passions, and live life on your own terms.
I hope you've achieved some of the simple dreams that mean so much to you: owning a car, creating a small cozy home filled with peace and comfort, and building a life that feels truly yours.
I hope you've read countless books, tried every sport you could, learned martial arts, stayed active, and continued challenging yourself to grow stronger both mentally and physically.
I hope you've never lost your creative side—the part of you that enjoys cooking, baking, crafting, and creating things with your own hands.
Most importantly, I hope you've remained kind, grateful, and true to yourself. I hope you've continued to embrace new experiences, learn from mistakes, and find joy in the journey rather than only the destination.
And if some of these dreams haven't happened yet, that's okay too. Keep moving forward. Keep believing in yourself. Keep becoming.
With hope and faith,
Mehak
